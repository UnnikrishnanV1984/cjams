Service Plan Candidacy Determination Criterias

  • Risk of Harm
    • Child came to the attention of the agency as a risk of harm case
    • Child is a substance exposed newborn
    • Child in a family with a prior child fatality and/or serious child injury
    • Child is under five years old and in a family with a previous CPS report
    • Child’s family was referred for ongoing services following a CPS risk of harm assessment in the last 12 months
    • Child in a family that experienced domestic violence in the family household within the last 12 months
  • Substance Use Disorder
    • Child is experiencing a substance use disorder
    • Caregiver of the child is experiencing a substance use disorder
    • Child’s family referred for ongoing services following a CPS investigation or alternative response assessment where substance use was a reason for the agency’s involvement
  • Victim of Trafficking
    • Child in the case is possible victim of trafficking (sex, human, labor)
    • Child’s CST/Trafficking Assessment has three or more Risk Factor boxes checked
  • Unsafe Living Conditions
    • Child’s family referred for ongoing services following a CPS investigation or alternative response assessment for allegations of neglect – unsafe living conditions
  • Complex Medical Needs
    • Child has an identified significant disability or medical/physical health challenge that requires specialized care to ensure the child’s health and safety
    • Child’s health tab identifies a complex medical need
    • Question 7 of the child’s current MFIRA or MFFRA identifies child as at least one of the following:
      • Medically fragile/failure to thrive
      • Positive toxicology at birth
      • Physical disability
    • Child has a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 in the medical/physical item of the Life Functioning domain
    • Child has a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 in the developmental/intellectual item of the Life Functioning domain
    • Caregiver has an identified significant disability or medical/physical health challenge
    • Caregiver has a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 in the medical/physical item of the Caregivers Needs and Resources domain
  • Complex Psychological or Behavioral Needs
    • Child has an identified complex psychological and/or behavioral need
    • Child has a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 for any of the items in the behavioral/emotional needs domain? These items include:
      • Psychosis
      • Impulsivity/Hyperactivity
      • Depression
      • Anxiety
      • Oppositional Conduct
      • Adjustment to Trauma
      • Attachment Difficulties
      • Anger Control
      • Substance Use
    • Caregiver has an identified complex psychological and/or behavioral need
    • Caregiver has a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 in the mental health item of the Caregivers Needs and Resources Domain and/or a CANS-F rating of at least a 2 in the developmental item of the Caregivers Needs and Resources Domain
  • Prior Child Welfare Experience
    • The child was part of a CPS investigation or AR within the last 12 months
    • The child’s family has past child welfare involvement
    • The child's family experienced a prior non-accidental injury to any child in the household
    • The child or any children in the case recently exited out of home care and achieved permanency, but is at risk of disruption and/or re-entry into foster care
    • The child is the sibling of a child/children in foster care, but resides at home and has assessed safety concerns
  • DJS Involvement
    • The child has current DJS involvement
    • The child has delinquency/DJS history
  • Informal Kinship
    • Child is living in an informal kinship living arrangement and will be served through in-home services. These are Kinship families who are not formally involved with the child welfare system and may need additional support to ensure that children can thrive and remain with their families.
  • Direct Order
    • A direct order is granted to a relative (or someone other than the local department) and the court case remains open (prevention candidate only)
  • None
    • If none is selected the family is not a Title IV-E prevention candidate eligible
