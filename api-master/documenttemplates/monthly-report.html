Title Instruction
Client Name Print/type client name
Client ID Number Print/type client ID #
Month/Year Indicate the Month and Year information on the form.
Provider/Vendor Name Indicate the Name of the Aide providing services and the name of their Agency.
Provider FEIN or SSN Applicable for vendor agencies.
Provider Street Address Street or mailing address
Provider City, State Zip Indicate city, state and zip code of the provider's agency.
Telephone Number Contact number for providers agency.
Project Category Code Indicate the Code the case is opened in as related to Client Informatin System.
Provider Type Indicate the type of provider: LDSS Parent Aide for parent aide services provided by the Local DSS, DSS Aide Service for all aide services provided by the
local DSS not considered parent aide services, Individual Provider for aide services provided to the client by a provider they have identified and have an
agreement for services and Vendor for any aide service provided by an aide employed by a Vendor Agency.
Aide Time In Aide indicates the time of arrival to the clients home on the day of the month represented under the block being filled in.
Tasks/Day of the Month Identifies the general categories of services the aide may provide and the day of the month those indicated services where performed.
Chore Only Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing or assisting with chores.
Personal Care Only Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing or assisting with personal care.
Heavy Chore Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing or assisting with heavy chore tasks.
Transporation/Escort General Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing or assisting with transporation to destinations not related to medical needs.
Transporation/Escort Medical Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing or assisting with transporation to destinations related to medical needs (doctor appt etc).
Therapeutic Parent Aide Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing therapeutic parent aide services to a families with children.
Therapeutic Adult Aide Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing therapeutic aide services to an adult client.
Respite Care Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing respite care to the client.
Title Instruction
Other Client Related Services Indicate in hours the time spent by the aide providing aide services that are not indicated the categories above.
Other Client Related Services Indicate in hours the total amount of time the aide spent that day providing all aide services combined.
Aide Travel Time Indicate in hours the total amount of time the aide spent traveling to and from the client home.
Aide Hours for Miscellaneous Indicate in hours the total amount of time the aide spent on that day completing tasks related to the clients case not related to direct client care.
(may include paperwork, training)
Indicate Codes for Tasks Performed Indicate in this space all tasks performed on that day with the client. (codes can be found on the back of the form or under
the Code list tab in the excel worksheet)
Aide Time Out Aide indicates the time the aide departed from the clients home on that day.
Client/Caregiver Signature Client or their caregiver signs the form each day aide service is provided to them indicating they are in agreement with the information
filled in on that day.
Invoice Section To populate this section the aide will transfer the information documented on the hard copy of the form with the clients signature to the
excel version of the form.
AIDE Signature After printing the completed excel form the aide will attach a copy of the coinsiding form that shows the clients signature and sign the
excel version prior to turning the form into their supervisor.
LDSS Supervisor Signature The local DSS IHAS supervisor will sign the form after verify that the services indicated match the services scheduled for the client during that month.
Fiscal Account Clerk Signature The local DSS Fiscal Officer will sign the form after verify the information on the form as applicable to that Local departments processing of
IHAS services.
