You have been placed under the supervision of the Community Detention (CD) Program. The CD Program is a statewide alternative to detention program that allows a youth to remain in the community on court-ordered home detention, as opposed to being placed in secure confinement pending a court appearance. Youth in the program are subject to enhanced treatment and supervision through electronic monitoring, face-to-face and telephone contacts by the Community Detention Officer (CDO). The Command Center provides additional electronic coverage twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week (24/7). The Community Detention Rapid Response (CD RR) Team is responsible for monitoring all youth on CD supervision on evenings and weekends. Their main purpose is to ensure youth on CD are abiding by program rules. This includes phone checks, home visits and requesting law enforcement assistance when needed. If you are non-compliant expect that you will be held accountable.

Under CD Supervision CD youth are permitted to leave their residence only to participate in :

  1. Educational programs
  2. Court hearings
  3. Scheduled meetings with their attorney
  4. Scheduled meetings with DJS case management staff
  5. Scheduled treatment appointment
  6. Activities pre-approved by the court; and/or
  7. Limited approved outings with custodian or guardian may be allowed (CD Outing Forms must be received 48 hours in advance and signed by parent/guardian).

** In addition, during inclement weather, during school closings or any unusual event, youth on the CD Program may be restricted to their home by the CDO.

Under CD Supervision a youth may be returned to Court or detained should you not adhere to the following :

  1. Abide by all CD Program Rules and remain in the home when not on an approved activity as stated above
  2. Abide by the conditions of the Court Order
  3. Attend all Court Hearings
  4. Not commit any new offenses

Parents are required to cooperate fully with the CD Program rules and notify the CDO of any infractions to this contract.
