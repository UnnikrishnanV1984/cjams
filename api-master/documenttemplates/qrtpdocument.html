MOTION TO AUTHORIZE CONTINUED PLACEMENT IN A QUALIFIED RESIDENTIAL TREATMENT PLACEMENT


The (LDSS County) Department of Social Services, hereafter LDSS, pursuant to Courts and Judicial Proceedings Article, Section 3-816.2(b), moves this Court to authorize the continued placement of the Respondent child in a Qualified Residential Treatment Program (QRTP).
In support of this Motion, the LDSS states that:
  • An assessment was conducted by a Qualified Individual who determined the QRTP is an appropriate placement for the child.
  • The child’s needs cannot be met through placement in a foster family home.
  • Placement in a QRTP is consistent with the short- and long-term goals specified in the child’s current permanency plan.
  • Placement in a QRTP is the most effective and appropriate level of care for the child in the least restrictive environment.

Wherefore, (LDSS) requests that the Court:
  • schedule this matter for an immediate hearing;
  • ratify the QRTP placement of the child;
  • find that the child’s needs cannot be met through placement in a foster family home;
  • find the placement in a QRTP provides the most effective and appropriate level of care for the child in the least restrictive environment;
  • schedule a further hearing on this Motion if required.
